## Derivation

- **Plan cap.** 162 plan output tokens per scene measured, against 1,500
  base plus 700 per scene — fits with fourfold headroom. The storyboard of
  a stand-in scene (idea, kit, three beats, share) is representative of a
  real scene's plan weight; re-run if storyboards grow execution fields.
- **Scene-length cap.** The svg scene's 59 s seek holds the 150 ms scrub
  limit at 23 ms, but the plot scene's reaches 726 ms: per-step plot
  resampling dominates, so capping length lower cannot fix it — even its
  20 s seek exceeds at 237 ms. The 60 s cap stands as the bound on
  replayed steps (at most 3,600); the video-figures section 6 contingency
  applies as follow-up work: seek from the start of the beat where a beat
  restarts the model from its values.
- **Scene counts.** One per 30 to 60 s of film (ADR 009): 1, 1, 2, 3, 4
  scenes for 30 s and 1, 2, 3, 4 min. Structural, confirmed by the run's
  storyboards validating at exactly those counts.
- **Deadlines.** Slowest scripted whole-film time per length (7 to 28 ms
  of checks, probe and speech queue) plus 19 s of model allowance (plan
  3 s, scene 12 s, one repair 4 s, from the figure bench) against 25, 45,
  90, 135 and 175 s — every length fits, and every deadline sits within
  the 180 s generation ceiling.
