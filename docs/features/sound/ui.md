# Sound: UI

- **Speaker button** on every figure that has a sound: off (crossed speaker) by default. One tap turns sound on for that figure and starts audio on the page. A long press or right click offers "Sound on for all figures on this page".
- **While playing:** the speaker icon shows three small bars that move with the total level, so a deaf or muted reader can see that sound is happening.
- **In the algebra view:** a `Sound` row shows its frequency and gain, and a small mute toggle.
- **In the graphics view:** sounds draw nothing by default. With `with show = true`, a tone draws a small speaker marker at a point the writer chooses, and its label shows the frequency.
- **Captions:** for accessibility, the figure's hidden object list reads each playing sound, for example "Tone a, 440 hertz, playing".
- **Studio settings:** master volume, and "Start with sound on" (off by default).
- **Mobile:** the speaker button is at least 44 by 44 points. A phone in silent mode still plays only if the reader turned sound on.
